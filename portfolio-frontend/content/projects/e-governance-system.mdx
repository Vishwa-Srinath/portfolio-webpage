## Project context

This university project digitalizes public-service management for Udubaddawa Pradeshiya Sabha and gives municipal teams a real-time view of their operations.

## System scope

- Mobile, web, and desktop applications for citizens and municipal staff
- IoT-based vehicle tracking using ESP32, SIM900, and NEO GPS hardware
- Cloud-backed operational data shared across the applications
- Machine-learning support for data-driven service management

## Technical scope

The system combines Python, machine learning, AWS, Flutter, Firebase, and embedded IoT hardware. Its goal is to improve service transparency, coordination between teams, and citizen engagement through one connected platform.
